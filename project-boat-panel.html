Smart Boat Panel

Sole Embedded & Hardware Engineer · OTTI s.r.l.s — Marine Electronics · Milan, Italy · May 2024 – Feb 2025

End-to-end redesign of the smart electrical panel system for sailing vessels under 24 m — the subject of my M.Sc. thesis at Politecnico di Milano. As the sole embedded and hardware engineer, I independently designed, validated, and wrote firmware for three production-intent PCBs. All boards are built around the ESP32, powered from 12 V / 24 V marine battery rails, and drawn in EasyEDA.

ESP32 ESP-IDF NMEA 2000 CAN Bus LVGL GSM / 4G LTE I²C · SPI · UART Buck Converter EasyEDA PCB Design Marine Electronics

Context & Problem

Traditional marine electrical panels rely on static wiring, fixed-logic toggles, and legacy analog gauges — difficult to maintain, impossible to monitor remotely, and fragile under marine vibration and salt air. OTTI s.r.l.s commissioned a full rearchitecture: intelligent switching with a hardware fail-safe, remote cellular monitoring via 4G/LTE, real-time vessel data over the NMEA 2000 bus, and modernised instrument readouts backed by circular OLEDs running LVGL.

System Architecture

Three custom PCBs communicate over a common inter-board bus. The Mainboard is the vessel brain: it handles GSM, RTC, and NMEA 2000. The Button Module provides physical load switching with a hardware-only fail-safe path — the relay operates correctly even if the MCU is offline. The LCD Module replaces analog gauges with high-resolution digital readouts via an 18-bit ADC and OLED display driven by LVGL.

PCB Modules

01

Mainboard — Vessel Brain

OTTI Mainboard Ver. 0.1 R2  ·  EasyEDA  ·  Two-layer square PCB

Central intelligence for the vessel: logs sensor data from the NMEA 2000 bus, provides remote cellular communication via an integrated 4G/LTE modem, and keeps accurate timestamps with a coin-cell backed RTC. Power input accepts raw 12–24 V marine battery rails and regulates down to 3.3 V logic and 4 V modem supplies.

  • MCU: ESP32 module (Wi-Fi + Bluetooth) with BOOT / RST buttons and USB flash port
  • Cellular: 4G/LTE SIM module with dedicated VIN → 4 V buck converter and external SMA antenna connector
  • NMEA 2000: CAN bus marine standard with 120 Ω termination and panel-mount M12 waterproof connector
  • RTC: Coin-cell battery holder for offline timekeeping
  • Power: 12–24 V marine input → 3.3 V logic rail + 4 V modem rail
  • Status: Discrete Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and link LEDs
02

Button Module — Hardware Fail-Safe Switch

OTTI Button Module Ver. 0.1 R3  ·  EasyEDA  ·  Compact two-layer PCB

Compact control node enabling physical load switching with a guaranteed hardware-only fail-safe path — the relay operates correctly even if the MCU is unpowered or mid-firmware update. A discrete latch/debounce logic circuit (MAX9516EUBL) sits between the momentary button and the N-MOSFET gate, ensuring reliable operation without any firmware dependency.

  • MCU: ESP32 with onboard antenna (Wi-Fi + Bluetooth)
  • Power: 12 V input → 3.3 V via synchronous buck converter (inductor + feedback network)
  • Load switching: SRA-12VDC-CL relay driven via N-MOSFET; flyback diode protects the coil
  • Fail-safe logic: MAX9516EUBL latch + HTC1600SW128 — panel usable without MCU
  • User I/O: Panel-mount push-button with integrated LED; discrete Wi-Fi / BT status LEDs
  • Programming: BD21A4N8 UART-USB bridge with JST connector for field firmware updates
  • Connectivity: Screw terminals for REL_NO / REL_COM / EL_COM; edge connector for inter-board communication
03

LCD Module — Digital Instrument Readout

OTTI LCD Module V0.1  ·  JLCPCB-002  ·  EasyEDA  ·  Compact red two-layer PCB

Replaces legacy analog voltage and current gauges with precise digital readouts. Two signal-conditioning channels scale raw sensor voltages via resistor dividers before feeding an 18-bit delta-sigma ADC. A digital potentiometer (I²C) allows programmable gain adjustment per installation. The ESP32 drives a circular OLED via LVGL for a clean, readable instrument face.

  • Power: AMS1117CD-3.3 LDO (12 V → 3.3 V) with 10 µF + 100 nF decoupling
  • ADC: MCP3424 — 4-channel, 18-bit delta-sigma over I²C; reads V1, V2, C1, C2 channels
  • Signal conditioning: Resistor dividers (24 kΩ / 9.1 kΩ) for raw sensor scaling; MCP4562 digital potentiometer for programmable gain
  • Display: FPC 1.25-2PWB flat-flex connector for OLED panel; rendered by LVGL on the host ESP32
  • Field terminals: Three 2-pin screw terminals for V1, V2, C1/C2; separate 12 V power terminal
  • Interface: 4-pin I²C header (SCL, SDA, 3V3, GND) to main controller

Panel Design & Renders

LVGL Display Interface

Legacy analog gauges were replaced with circular OLEDs running LVGL. The UI renders real-time voltage, current, and status data drawn from the 18-bit ADC via I²C — eliminating mechanical gauge failure modes and enabling fully configurable readout layouts.

PCB & Electronics

Installation & Prototype

Prototype boards were assembled and tested aboard actual sailing vessels, validating the design under real marine conditions — 12 V / 24 V battery rails, vibration, and high-humidity salt-air environments.
